OvenHire terms and demo disclaimer

Demo preview · Last updated 24 November 2026

OvenHire is an early concept and software demonstration. It is not currently a live recruitment marketplace, employment agency, staffing provider, immigration adviser or payment service.

Sample vacancies

All sample roles and employers shown in this preview are illustrative examples, not verified vacancies or offers of employment. Do not rely on them to make career, travel or financial decisions.

Demo workspaces

Job drafts, candidate profile details and demo applications are stored only in your browser using local storage. They are not submitted to an employer and are not visible to other users. Browser data may be lost or accessible to other people using the same device profile.

Pricing and payments

Any ₹100 or ₹200 prices shown are proposed future pricing only. No payment can or should be made through this preview. No job placement, interview, salary or visa sponsorship is guaranteed.

Safe use

Never pay a recruiter for a guaranteed job or visa, and never share OTPs, banking PINs or passwords. Do not upload or enter sensitive personal data.

Production status

Real accounts, database, live listings, moderation, employer checks, applications, CV storage, customer support, refunds, and payment processing are not yet implemented. These terms are a plain-language product notice, not a substitute for legal review.

OvenHire privacy notice

Demo preview notice · Last updated 24 November 2026

This website is an early front-end demonstration, not a live recruitment service. It does not provide user accounts, server-side candidate profiles, CV uploads, live applications, payment checkout or employer verification.

Information stored in your browser

If you use the demo workspaces, the browser may save the information you type (such as a demo profile, local job draft or demo application) in this device's local storage. That information is not sent to an OvenHire server by this prototype. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view it. Avoid entering sensitive, confidential or identifying information.

Cookies and analytics

This preview does not intentionally set advertising cookies or include analytics tracking. Your hosting provider and browser may process basic technical request data under their own terms.

Sharing

The share buttons open your device's native share interface or copy a link. You choose whether and where to share it.

Children and sensitive data

This preview is not designed for children and should not be used to submit identity documents, financial details, health information or CVs.

Contact

No dedicated privacy contact channel has been configured for this prototype. Do not submit private information through this site.

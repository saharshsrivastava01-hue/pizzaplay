Concept first. Kitchen next.
Launch with proof.

01 / PARTNER

Secure a licensed operating kitchen and agree production, quality and delivery responsibilities.

02 / PILOT

Test a small menu, food cost, packaging, preparation time and customer feedback.

03 / LIST

04 / VENUE

Plan the physical immersive café only after demand, funding, permissions and unit economics are proven.

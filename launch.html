Launch campaign concepts

Three local stories worth sharing

01 · Neighbourhood food discovery

“A new pizza name in Amdavad”

Ask local food pages to review the actual menu after kitchen launch. Offer a transparent tasting invite only when the kitchen and budget are ready; disclose paid or complimentary collaborations.

02 · Office and student group orders

“Build a shared pizza table”

Show a real menu bundle and verified per-person price. Share in relevant office, college or housing communities only with group-admin approval.

03 · Customer-created content

“Show us your first slice”

Invite customers to post an honest photo or short reel and tag the brand. Obtain permission before reusing any photo, name or video.

LoanPath · Application preparation

Prepare a loan application — keep control of every submission

Choose a loan type and the lenders you want to research. We will prepare a lender checklist and send you to each lender's official website. You complete the application, consent, KYC and final submission yourself.

No mass applications: opening many bank accounts does not guarantee a loan. Each lender independently checks your income, occupation, current debt and credit history. Applying to many lenders close together can create multiple hard enquiries and may affect your CIBIL score. Start by comparing, then apply only where the published eligibility appears to fit.

The location and purpose stay on this page only; they are not submitted.

Loan preparation checklist

  • Check lender eligibility and whether the product accepts your employment/business profile.
  • Have the bank's required identity/address documents ready, but enter them only on the bank's own secure site or at its branch.
  • For salaried applicants, the lender may request salary slips, employment details and bank statements.
  • For self-employed applicants, the lender may request ITRs, financial statements and business proof.
  • Check existing EMIs and total monthly affordability before borrowing.
  • Never use false salary, employment, business or address details.

Compare the Key Facts Statement (KFS)

For covered retail and MSME term loans, RBI requires lenders to give prospective borrowers a standardised KFS. Compare the APR (the annual all-in cost), processing fees, net disbursal, EMI schedule, late/penal charges and prepayment conditions—not just the headline interest rate.

Before applying

Safe application checklist

  • Open the bank website from this directory and check that the address is exactly the official domain.
  • Find that bank’s current savings/current-account product page and its eligibility rules.
  • Read the latest minimum-balance, debit-card, SMS, ATM, cash-deposit and closure charges.
  • Confirm which KYC method and documents the bank currently accepts.
  • Complete identity checks and video KYC yourself, only inside the bank’s official app/site or branch.
  • Read all declarations and submit only information that is accurate.
  • Save the bank’s application reference privately, not in this guide.
RBI rule: KYC is mandatory when a customer starts an account-based relationship with a regulated entity. The bank is responsible for identity verification. Read RBI's KYC FAQ ↗
Stay safe

Never give these to an agent

Never share: OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV, netbanking password, full card number or remote-screen-control access.

Do not upload identity documents to this assistant. Do not pay a person who promises guaranteed account approval or asks for a “processing fee” through a personal UPI ID.

Use the bank's official customer-care details from its own website. Report suspected digital payment fraud promptly through the official national cybercrime helpline 1930 and website cybercrime.gov.in.

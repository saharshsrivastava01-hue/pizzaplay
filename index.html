Before you order

Is this a live online payment checkout?

No. This website sends an order request. It does not charge a card, UPI account or wallet. Pay only through a method confirmed directly by the business.

Are ingredients and allergen details verified?

Not yet. The displayed menu is a configurable sample. Contact the kitchen before ordering with allergies or dietary requirements; cross-contact may occur.

Where is delivery available?

Naranpura and Navrangpura are initial target neighbourhoods, not a live verified delivery map. The store must confirm each address, delivery availability, fee and timing.

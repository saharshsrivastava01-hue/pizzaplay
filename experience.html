The oven ritual

Four acts.
One great slice.

A premium experience is built through repeatable kitchen craft—not just a beautiful screen. The actual team must test and approve each stage.

01 — PREPARE

The dough

Weigh, portion and proof consistently. Record the dough formula and fermentation conditions.

02 — COMPOSE

The balance

Measure sauce, cheese and toppings so the centre stays light and the crust remains crisp.

03 — FIRE

The bake

Calibrate time and temperature to the real oven. Check the base, rim and centre every test batch.

04 — FINISH

The handoff

Inspect, slice and pack. Test the pizza after a realistic delivery journey before publishing claims.
